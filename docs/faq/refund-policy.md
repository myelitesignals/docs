# Refund Policy

We don't offer refunds. Here's exactly what that means for each product, and what we do instead.

## EliteAlgo Memberships

### Refunds

EliteAlgo memberships are not refundable once a billing period has been charged.

### Cancellation

You can cancel anytime in one click via the Portal at [**portal.elitesignals.com**](https://portal.elitesignals.com). You won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

### Duplicate Payments

If you're ever charged twice by mistake, we'll refund the extra charge. Email [**support@elitesignals.com**](mailto:support@elitesignals.com) with the details of the duplicate transaction.

## Elite X

### All Sales Final

Elite X is sold as a one-time lifetime license, so all Elite X sales are final and we don't offer refunds. If you have any questions, ask us before you buy at [**support@elitesignals.com**](mailto:support@elitesignals.com). After purchase, we help you get set up and stay available for ongoing support.

## Need Help?

If something isn't working for you, email [**support@elitesignals.com**](mailto:support@elitesignals.com) or use the live chat on our website and we'll help you sort it out.
